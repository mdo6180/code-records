How to build the setpoint generators for theta_d
T_x = T * cos(θ)
T_z = T * sin(θ)

T_x = m * a_x,d
T_z = mg

T_x / T_z = m * a_x,d / mg = a_x,d / g

θ_d = tan^-1(a_x,d / g)
