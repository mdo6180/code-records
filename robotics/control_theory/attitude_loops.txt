| Symbol     | Name                | Meaning                                               | Typical units |
| ---------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| (\phi_d)   | Desired roll angle  | How far the drone should roll left/right              | rad or °      |
| (\theta_d) | Desired pitch angle | How far the drone should pitch nose-up/down           | rad or °      |
| (\psi_d)   | Desired yaw angle   | Which compass/heading direction the drone should face | rad or °      |
| (p_d)      | Desired roll rate   | How fast it should rotate around body (x)             | rad/s         |
| (q_d)      | Desired pitch rate  | How fast it should rotate around body (y)             | rad/s         |
| (r_d)      | Desired yaw rate    | How fast it should rotate around body (z)             | rad/s         |
| (\tau_x)   | Roll torque         | Torque around body (x)                                | N·m           |
| (\tau_y)   | Pitch torque        | Torque around body (y)                                | N·m           |
| (\tau_z)   | Yaw torque          | Torque around body (z)                                | N·m           |
