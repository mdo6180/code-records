Hierarchy of a drone controller:

            Trajectory Planner
                    │
                    │ z_desired, v_desired, a_desired (i.e., next waypoint along trajectory)
                    ▼
    Altitude, Roll, Pitch, Yaw Controllers
                    │
                    │ desired thrust / roll, pitch, yaw torques
                    ▼
Motor Controller / Motor Mixer / Control Allocator
                    │
                    │ pwm signals
                    ▼
                Drone Motors
                    │
                    │ z, v
                    └──────── feedback to Controllers
